### Our interpretation and explanation of why this quotation is relevant (max. 800 characters) (textarea, required)

```
The paper relates the loss of open water at Akgöl to temperature and precipitation records, and reads the 2017 rebound as an effect of precipitation, the wetland's only feeding source. Whether the decline follows the weather or water use matters for management: if it is climate, little can be done locally; if it is abstraction for irrigation, water allocation can change it. The replication tests the climate explanation with a basin water balance from reanalysis and with the wetlands' filling in comparably wet years.
```
